AI in Grid Planning: Increase Resilience, Expedite Decisions, and Lower Costs

Discover how AI-enabled grid planning, powered by As utilities face growing demand, renewable energy integration, interconnection backlogs, and increasing grid complexity, traditional planning methods can struggle to keep pace. AI-enabled grid planning helps utilities evaluate future scenarios, improve forecasting accuracy, identify potential risks, and make faster, more informed decisions while supporting reliable and affordable electricity.

GE Vernova’s GridOS® portfolio provides a platform-based approach to AI-enabled grid planning, bringing together trusted network models, geospatial intelligence, operational data, and AI-driven analytics. With an accurate, continuously updated digital representation of the grid—a grid twin—utilities can better understand network constraints, assess future conditions, and align long-term planning with day-to-day operations.
